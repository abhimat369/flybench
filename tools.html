DILUTION / BENCH WORKSHEET

Prepare a dilution

Choose the stock, target and final-volume units. The result uses your selected volume unit.

RESULTEnter your values

THE FORMULAV₁ = C₂ × V₂ / C₁

This assumes an additive-volume dilution. V₁ uses your selected volume unit; the concentration units can differ. It is not a vapor-concentration calculator.

MASS / BENCH WORKSHEET

Weigh a solution component

Calculate the solute mass from molecular weight, target concentration and final solution volume.

RESULTEnter your values

THE FORMULAmass (mg) = MW × mM × mL / 1000

Use the molecular weight of the actual chemical form, including hydration. Dissolve first, then bring the solution to its final volume.

ST50 / BENCH WORKSHEET

Interpolate an ST50

Enter two observations that bracket 50% for the same defined endpoint. Use minutes for both times.

RESULTEnter your values

THE FORMULAST50 = t₁ + (50 − p₁)(t₂ − t₁)/(p₂ − p₁)

No extrapolation is performed. If a curve does not reach 50%, report the endpoint as unreached within the observation period. Placeholder numbers are illustrative, not experimental data.

TOLERANCE / BENCH WORKSHEET

Compare challenge ST50 values

Compare ethanol-preexposed and age-matched naive cohorts under the same challenge and endpoint.

RESULTEnter your values

THE FORMULAchange (%) = 100 × (preexposed − naive) / naive

The result is a descriptive percentage change. It does not establish a mechanism or replace a comparison of independent biological replicates.

AIRFLOW / BENCH WORKSHEET

Separate flow fraction from ethanol concentration

Calculate the proportion of mixed carrier flow that came through each bubbler branch.

RESULTEnter your values

THE FORMULAbranch fraction = Qethanol / (Qethanol + Qwater)

The output is the ethanol-bubbler branch’s share of carrier flow. It is NOT airborne ethanol % v/v. Gas composition also depends on vapor generation, temperature, saturation and the apparatus.
